Just arcada games to practice. But put them in a tycoon game like roller coaster tycoon.

Queue points:

After using an arcade machine, it generates a queue. Each person in the queue gives you money, basically it's like “opening” a game to generate money. Then to play it again you have to wait for the cool down, i.e. queue. Use queue points to skip the queue. Individual places

Special objectives in the mini games gives you queue points . For example in mash the moles, getting a passing mosquito

In your main screen, you have your arcade layout. You can buy “halls” to expand, or doors and walls to divide.

On the walls, in slots, you can put an arcade machine.

Pressing on the machine zooms in, and you see info about it. Pressing play, you enter game mode. alternatively, swipe left or right to see next machine, and you can swipe “into” coordiors to navigate

Each arcade machine has a set price, time to play, etc. enter queue point here.

occassionaly, a person will show up at the counter. they may request one of your machines if htey’re a business man, or they may request a series of copies of the game, which you have to retrieve manually (download to usb) to then hand to them. They will ask for copies of the most successfull games.

How to make a game more successful? better games are more expensive, first of all. A game can be enhanced by placing decorations, and has a “slot” bonus it can fill. A special, magical decoration can be like a riven mod to a given game.

The player wants to make certain, more profitable games, “better”, in order to sell copies at the shop.

Screens:

1. Door (open/close)
    1. Can open and close the shop
2. Counter
    1. Address customer requests, access other menus
3. Arcade slot
    1. Face arcade for info
    2. Add new arcade slot / new hall expansion
    3. By default, a room has 4 slots. Buy a hall expansion to go further, or buy a door to open a new “group” of arcades.
    4. Change arcade price for ticket - this will gauge how many people will play it or queue for it.
4. Door
    1. tap to enter, continue to go continue on the hall (go back to start at end)

putting down new arcades, achieving certain goals, expanding the store, etc gains you XP, at level up you get rewards and research points.
