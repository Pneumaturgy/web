# Rapha's Story

> Status: story proposal · created 23 July 2024
> Source: Notion, *Projects → Pneuma Knights → Rapha's Story*.
> Parent: [Por Nada](por-nada.md)

Quick summary:

- Fooled protagonist fight for the enemy without knowing
- Small tips on the gameplay of what's going on
- Protagonist discovers truth and fight back

Concept:
You find yourself as a fighter against  beings that invaded your homeland trying to retrieve its glory by recovering artifacts.

You enter a Virtual Reality equipment that allows you to control a soldier to fight in the planet, everytime you die as the soldier you come back to the room where you can reenter the loop until all the artifacts are retrieved.

Upon exploring the world you encounter with yourself, that seems to use the same equipments as previous versions of you, but they desperatelly try to kill you.

There's always some being in the base praising you and telling what to do next, encouraging you in a suspicious manner to keep it going.

When you kill an apparent older version of you, they express sentiments as if they were trying to communicate with you...

Those small tips, start guiding you by older versions pointing to you one direction before dying.

When following the directions, you find an old city where clues about what is going on is written on diaries/wall paints/etc.

By it's various lifes the player learn that it is in reality a citizen of that world that suffered brainwarsh to help an external empire colonize the planet, and has being fighting for them all this time.

The relics are in reality powerful artifacts that can be used to build a weapon that would allow this empire to obliterate the entire lifeform of planets.

This relics are usually stored in very guarded places pointed by the suspicious guide in the base, and seems to be used as a source of power.

When discovering that, the player can explore a little more the base, ignore some rules and start fighting back...

It encounters and partners with the resistant army to destry the empire from the inside
