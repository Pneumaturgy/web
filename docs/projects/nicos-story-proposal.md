# Nico's Story Proposal

> Status: story proposal · created 22 July 2024
> Source: Notion, *Projects → Pneuma Knights → Nico's Story Proposal*.
> Parent: [Pneuma Knights](pneuma-knights.md)

*Our story opens with a silent cut scene in which* Mr. Smith *(our protagonist, working name)* wakes up in a drop pod falling from orbit onto a strange planet. As it falls, it goes through the normal reentry physics for any object leaving orbit *(parabolic arc, super heated atmosphere, red hot craft, etc -- think reentry in [KSP](https://store.steampowered.com/app/220200/Kerbal_Space_Program/))* before finally firing thrusters at the last possible moment to de-accelerate and hit the ground softly.

The pod sits there for a few seconds, completely quiet *(we have yet to see our main character)* and then the first sounds we hear are that of a man exerting all of his strength as we see a mech rip a hole in the side of the pod and emerge onto the service of a strange vibrant planet… *think Neon colored Jungle*. This mech, scratched, faded and battletorn, juxtaposed against the pristine landscape behind him emits a few words:

**Who am I? What am I doing here?**

Then, as if by instinct among his confusion, he reaches into the pod and pulls out a weapon *(cutscene should change based on starting weapon)*

*In this world, aliens that look like they could be plants will often attack the protagonist, especially at night time **(day/night cycle is 10 minutes long, so one of these comes up around once every 10 minutes, until later in the game when plants attack during the day at which point it might be every 5 minutes or even closer together as the game gets more intense)** to which he responds with a few different quotes, with a pool that increases over time. Quotes are picked randomly and we never repeat one of the last 5 quotes said (none of this resets on death):*

*0 or more quotes have been said:*

- **Damn plants… Why did I have to leave my flamethrower at home?**
    - *At 20 or more quotes **or once you find a flamethrower**, this no longer triggers.*
- **I hate this stupid planet.**
- **I can’t wait for these plants to go back to bed.** *(we should go through at least one full day/night cycle for this one to trigger, ie from night 2 onwards)*
- I can’t wait for these bugs to go back to bed
- **Burn in hell, you little flowers!**
- **Where are reinforcements when you need them?**
    - *At 20 or more quotes, this no longer triggers.*

*5 or more quotes have been said:*

- **Why does this world feel so familiar? I can’t put my finger on it.**
- **Dammit <name>, why did you have to die on me?** *(we need to have found at least one dead mech for this one to trigger)*
    - *As more dead mechs are found, the list of names that could be selected grows. Each dead mech secretly has an associated name that gets added to the pool of names.*
- **Always, the Corporation has to give such dumb and tedious assignments.**
    - *At 20 or more quotes, this becomes:*
        - **If someone is still alive at the Corporation, I want my vacation time.**
- **I don’t get paid enough for this work.**
- **Fortunately these plants aren’t smart enough to unionize on me.**

*10 or more quotes have been said:*

- **My comrades got what they deserved.** *(we need to have found at least one dead mech for this one to trigger) -- at 15 or more quotes, this becomes:*
    - ***My dead comrades got what they deserved.***
        - *At 20 or more quotes, this becomes:*
            - **I killed my comrades. They got what they deserved.**
                - *At 25 or more quotes, this becomes:*
                    - **I burned my comrades to death alive. They got what they deserved.**
- **I kill at the pleasure of the Corporation.**
    - *At 15 or more quotes, this becomes:*
        - **I kill for pleasure.**
- **All the treehuggers must die for the Corporation!**
- **Come at me, bro!**
- **An order is an order.**

*15 or more quotes have been said:*

- **I killed them, I killed them all.** *(we need to have found at least one dead mech for this one to trigger)*
- **They will never change me.**
    - *At 30 or more quotes, this becomes:*
        - **The Corporation will never change me.**
- **Give me fire support.** *(we need to have found at least one automatically firing weapon for this one to trigger)*
    - *At 20 or more quotes, this becomes:*
        - ***Give me fire support… Oh wait, you can’t… because you’re all dead.***
- **Burn baby burn.**
    - *At 20 or more quotes, this becomes:*
        - **Burn baby burn, just like my fallen comrades.**
- **Is this world real?** *(we need to have died for this to trigger)*
    - *After this has been said 3 times, the 4th time and beyond on it becomes:*
        - **This world isn’t real.**

*After exploring for a while (every 2-5 days, but only if we are seeing brand new territory), the mech stumbles upon a dead mech half covered in dirt and a new cutscene plays:*

Our protagonist approaches the mech, which looks awfully like our protagonist. He smashes in the windshield like some sort of barbarian and pulls out a data chip, plugging it into himself, eager to see the logs of his fallen comrade.

In the logs, you get one of the following messages, read by our protagonist:

- **Day X: Another day of aliens trying to kill me on this god forsaken planet.** *(X is the number of days that have passed +/- 10%, the day count **does not reset** on death)*
    - *On the second time this triggers, it becomes:*
        - ***Day X: Another day of aliens trying to kill me on this god forsaken planet. Ran into another former comrade who tried to snuff me out, but I got the better of him and took his gear.***
        - *On the third time this triggers, it becomes:*
            - **Day X: Another day of aliens trying to kill me on this god forsaken planet. Ran into another former comrade who tried to snuff me out, but I got the better of him and took his gear. *No one disrespects me. Let them all burn in hell.***
            - *On the fourth time and beyond that this triggers, it becomes:*
                - ***Day X: Another day of aliens trying to kill me on this god forsaken planet. Ran into another former comrade who tried to snuff me out, but I got the better of him and took his gear. No one disrespects me. Let them all burn in hell just like everyone else who has ever crossed me.***
- **Day Y: I remembered why I am here. Soon the Corporation will come for me.** *(Y is the number of days that have passed +10% to +30%, the day count **does reset** on death. Y is at least 10.)*
- **Day Z: I killed them. I killed them all. They got what they deserved, but now I must pay the price.** *(Z is the number of days that have passed -10% to -50%, the day count **does reset** on death)*

*After exploring for a longer period of time (every 10-15 days), the protagonist stumbles upon another mech that is alive. That mech will fight other aliens in its presence until it detects you at which point it will make you its priority. Its voice will be a modulated copy of the protagonist's voice and say something from your pool of available quotes, picked randomly among the last five quotes spoken by the protagonist.*

*Every 3rd, 4th or 5th mech that is alive that you encounter will be a tougher fight and on death will provide a rarer/more powerful part. Here we reuse the cutscene for finding a dead mech, but now the possible messages are:*

- **Day X: I think I am trapped in a simulation.** *(X is the number of days that have passed +/- 10%, the day count **does reset** on death)*
    - *After the first time, this becomes:*
        - **Day X: I *have confirmed that* I am trapped in a simulation.**
- **Day Y: I know how to escape now. Soon the Corporation will come for me.** *(Y is the number of days that have passed +10% to +30%, the day count **does not reset** on death. Y is at least 10.)*
- **Day Z: I am here for what I have done. They want to reprogram me. That is my punishment.** *(Z is the number of days that have passed -10% to -50%, the day count **does not reset** on death)*
- ***Day X: They want to reset me to continue their sick experiments. But I will never again forget what happened.** (X is the number of days that have passed +/- 10%, the day count **does reset** on death)*

*Every 2nd or 3rd harder mech will contain a “holy” item that you don’t have yet that is much more powerful than anything else. Collect a complete set of “holy” items (one for each character slot) and we get shown the following cut scene:*

*Our protagonist is a human in an orange jumpsuit, asleep in a human size pod, his head completely bald and wired up to the top of the pod with a bunch of cables, underneath the pod, there is a plaque that reads:*

***Experiment #0052 in progress. Warning: do not disturb, known serial killer and pyromaniac. Traitor to the Corporation.***

*After 15 seconds or so, the human’s eyelids flutter open, the subject is awake and angry, bloodlust in its eyes. He smashes the glass with his fists and rips out the cables attached to his head, jumps out of the pod onto the nearest guard, pulling the guard’s weapon out of his holster and kills the guard before the guard even knows what is happening. The cutscene ends and now you play as a human trying to escape this prison filled with glass pods and subjects wired up, asleep in their own simulations with guards and scientists walking around observing them. Each plaque below a subject announces different crimes committed by that individual against the Corporation. In the background, you hear the scientists discussing each case, including yours. Once you escape the jail, the player is congratulated on beating the game along with being shown their stats, the credits roll and a final (epilogue) cutscene plays where you are running to freedom, only to get shot by a guard, and your body dragged back into the jail. Over your body one scientist says to another:*

*Scientist 1:* **This one escaped again.**

*Scientist 2:* **This subject is dangerous, isn’t it time we put *it* out of its misery? That is the Xth time it has tried to escape this month. One of these days, it is going to kill us.** *(X is a random number between 2 and 5)*

*Scientist 1:* **You bring that up everytime. But we are expendable to the Corporation, so they would never let us kill this experiment. Just increase the difficulty of its simulation by 10% and get it back in there.**

*Scientist 2 (begrudgingly):* **Yes sir. An order is an order.**

*And with that, the game resets (playing the prologue cutscene) and is now a higher difficulty (this is communicated to the player).*
