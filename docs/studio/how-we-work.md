# How we work

> Source: Notion, *Welcome To Pneumaturgy* → "How we work".

## Cadence

We meet two times a week, currently on Tuesday and Wednesday. Occasionally,
we may meet on the weekend for an event, an educational meeting, or simply
to hang out.

## Circular pairing

When working together, we do circular pairing; that is one navigator and one
driver, and as features are finished, a new driver is chosen and the
previous driver becomes the navigator.
